Make Your Case: Win an Argument With Evidence

Have you ever lost an argument you knew you were right about? This lesson shows you how to build an argument that's hard to beat, whether you're in a debate round, writing an essay, or asking for a later curfew.

Who this lesson is for

Middle and high school students (grades 7–10) who are new to debate. You don't need any experience. The whole lesson takes about 2–3 class periods.

What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  1. Write a claim that takes a clear side on a debatable question.
  2. Support your claim with evidence and reasoning (Claim–Evidence–Reasoning).
  3. Answer the other side by rebutting at least one counterargument.
  4. Present your argument in a clear, organized way.

How to use this site

  1. Go to Learn the skills. For each part, choose the way that works best for you: read it, see and hear it, or try it. You can mix and match.
  2. Follow along with the worked example on phones in school to see every step done for you.
  3. Show what you learned in the Make Your Case assessment. You choose the format: speech, writing, visual, or live debate.

Choose your path

  • 📖 Read it

    Short, step-by-step explanations with key words highlighted. Best if you like to go at your own pace and re-read.

  • 🎧 See and hear it

    Diagrams plus an audio walkthrough with a full transcript. Best if you learn from pictures or listening.

  • ✋ Try it

    Practice sorting, spotting, and building arguments, with answers you can check. Best if you learn by doing.
