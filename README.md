# Debate Skills

Course materials for a debate skills lesson, built around two sets of design guidelines:

- **[Accessibility checklist](docs/accessibility-checklist.md)** — how every page, image, video, and link on the course site should be built.
- **[Assessment guidelines](docs/assessment-guidelines.md)** — how summative assessments are designed, including learner choice, instructions, alignment to objectives, and grading criteria.
- **[Summative assessment: Make Your Case](docs/summative-assessment.md)** — a ready-to-use assessment that applies those guidelines.
- **[Draft lesson site](site/index.html)** — an accessible, multi-page draft of the lesson to rebuild in Google Sites.
- **[UDL site guide](docs/udl-site-guide.md)** — how the draft site in `site/` meets the UDL assignment, plus step-by-step instructions for the parts done in Google (making the copy, Creative Commons photos, sharing Drive files).

## Draft site pages

`site/index.html` (Home), `site/learn.html` (Learn: three approaches per skill), `site/example.html` (Worked Example), `site/assessment.html`, `site/about.html`, `site/credits.html`, plus `site/transcript.html` and `site/worksheet.html`.
