Summative Assessment: Make Your Case

This assessment measures whether you can build, support, and defend an argument on a debatable topic.

Learning objectives

  1. Construct a claim that takes a clear position on a debatable resolution.
  2. Support the claim with reasoning and credible evidence.
  3. Anticipate and rebut at least one counterargument.
  4. Communicate the argument in an organized way for a specific audience.

Instructions

  1. Choose a resolution from the class list, or propose your own.
  2. Choose your side and write a one-sentence claim.
  3. Find at least two credible sources and note why each is credible.
  4. Build at least two supporting points using Claim–Evidence–Reasoning.
  5. Identify the strongest counterargument and write a rebuttal.
  6. Create your final product in the format you chose.
  7. Check your work with the self-check checklist.
  8. Submit your work and source list in the learning management system by the due date.

Rubric criteria (5 points each, 20 total)

  • Claim: clear, specific position on the resolution.
  • Evidence and reasoning: credible, cited sources, with reasoning that connects them to the claim.
  • Counterargument and rebuttal: the strongest opposing point, stated fairly and answered.
  • Organization and delivery: logical structure that fits the format and audience.
